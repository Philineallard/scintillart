# Final approval checklist

Before anything is published, confirm:

- Is it immediately clear what is being offered?
- Is the artist or work the focus?
- Are all names, dates, prices and credits correct?
- Are permissions and usage rights confirmed?
- Does the language avoid unsupported claims?
- Is the text readable on a phone?
- Does the design use approved colours and typography?
- Are cropping and interface overlays checked?
- Is there one clear next step?
- Does it feel specific to Scintillart rather than generically luxurious?

**The governing rule:** Art first. Clarity always. Quality in the details.
