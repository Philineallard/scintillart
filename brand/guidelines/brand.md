# Brand essence, positioning and services

## Our definition

**Scintillart** /sɪnˈtɪl.ɑːrt/ — noun

1. **A creative spark made tangible through art.** The initial flash of an idea, feeling, image, or encounter, given form, shared, and allowed to travel.
2. **A place or practice where small creative impulses become lasting work.** A studio, retreat, gathering, or collaboration in which people make space for curiosity, craft, and connection.
3. **The visible trace of an artistic exchange.** Not only the artwork itself, but the atmosphere, conversation, relationship, and momentum it leaves behind.

This is a coined brand definition, not a conventional dictionary entry.

## Purpose, mission, vision, promise

- **Purpose.** To bring an artist's perspective into the places, experiences and stories people remember.
- **Mission.** We connect artists with hospitality, brands and private clients to create considered experiences, cultural programmes and commissioned work.
- **Vision.** A world in which art is not simply displayed, but experienced, participated in and lived with.
- **Promise.** Distinctive artists. Thoughtful direction. Considered delivery.

## Positioning

Scintillart is an artist agency and creative production partner. We develop artist-led experiences, retreats, performances, exhibitions and creative commissions for hospitality, brands and private clients.

- **Short description.** Scintillart brings artists, people and places together through experiences and creative work.
- **Standard description.** Scintillart is an artist agency creating experiences, cultural programmes and commissioned work for hospitality, brands and private clients. From intimate workshops and retreats to photography, campaigns and exhibitions, we bring together the right artists, a clear creative direction and attention to every detail.

### What makes us different

- Artistic practice comes before decorative entertainment.
- Each artist is selected for the project, audience and setting.
- Creative ambition is supported by practical production.
- Quality is expressed through the work and the experience, not inflated language.
- Artists are collaborators whose authorship, time and rights are respected.

### Positioning boundary

Scintillart is not a generic activity marketplace, an unrestricted creative directory or a wellness brand. Wellbeing may support an experience; artistic practice remains its centre.

## Audience

Working audience priorities, not validated demographic personas.

| Audience | What they seek | What we communicate |
|---|---|---|
| Design-led hotels and resorts | Distinctive guest programming and creative content | Artist selection, relevance to place, dependable delivery |
| Event producers and brand teams | Original ideas and professional execution | Creative direction, production clarity, audience fit |
| Private guests | Meaningful access to artists in a considered setting | Artist, process, atmosphere, comfort and practical details |
| Private hosts and cultural clients | Personalised artistic experiences and selections | Discretion, thoughtful curation and individual attention |
| Artist collaborators | Relevant opportunities and fair working relationships | Clear briefs, authorship, agreed fees and professional support |

**Audience rule.** Speak to the buyer's purpose. A hotel proposal explains delivery and guest relevance; a retreat invitation communicates the artist, experience and setting. Neither relies on the word "luxury" to explain its value.

## Services

One master brand with four service categories.

- **Experiences & Retreats.** Artist-led workshops, private sessions and multi-day retreats.
- **Hospitality & Events.** Guest programmes, performances, residencies, cultural programming and artistic events.
- **Editorial & Campaigns.** Photography, writing, art direction and creative commissions across hospitality, fashion, travel and culture.
- **Exhibitions & Art.** Artist selections, temporary exhibitions, portfolio presentations and artwork commissions.

### Naming rules

- Use Scintillart as the primary identity; service names are navigation labels, not separate brands.
- Use "Scintillart presents" for specific programmes or collaborations.
- Describe only services we can confidently deliver, and distinguish completed projects from proposed formats.
- Use "represented artist" only where a representation agreement exists.
- The retreat is named "The Spark": always with "The", always capitalised as a name. The slogan's wording ("a spark") is unchanged.
