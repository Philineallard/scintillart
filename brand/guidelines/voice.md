# Slogans, messaging and voice

## Master slogan

Where a spark becomes art.

Use "a spark" in the slogan, not "the spark". Do not rewrite or rotate the master slogan between applications. Use it selectively, not as a compulsory footer on every post.

## The retreat name

The retreat is called **The Spark**. Always keep "The" and write it as a name with capitals. It is separate from the master slogan: do not use "The Spark" as a slogan or as another name for Scintillart, and do not change the slogan's "a spark" to match it.

## Functional descriptor

Artist-led experiences & creative services. Use it when the audience needs an immediate explanation of the business.

### Usage rules

- Use one headline or slogan per composition; do not stack several poetic lines.
- Pair expressive headlines with concrete information.
- Avoid promises of transformation or outcomes we cannot substantiate.

## Voice

Cultured, warm, precise and assured. We sound like people who understand art and know how to deliver a project, not like a luxury advertisement.

### Writing rules

- Clear international English and consistent British spelling.
- Prefer active verbs and specific nouns; keep paragraphs short.
- Name the artist, discipline, place and format whenever possible.
- Describe what happens before describing how it might feel.
- Use "curated" only when there is genuine selection and a clear rationale.
- Avoid unnecessary superlatives and exclamation marks.

**Preferred language:** artist-led, considered, commissioned, material, practice, perspective, encounter, collaboration, place, process.

**Avoid:** exclusive luxury, unforgettable journey, unleash your creativity, elevate your senses, world-class, magical, hidden gem, something for everyone.

### Examples

Instead of "An unforgettable luxury journey into creativity." write "Three days of photography and printmaking with working artists, hosted in a private coastal house."

Instead of "We provide premium artistic solutions." write "We develop artist-led workshops, performances and exhibitions for hotels and private events."

### Calls to action

Discuss a project · Request a proposal · Explore the experience · View the programme · Meet the artists · Enquire about a private session. Do not use invitation or application language unless participation genuinely requires it.
