# Visual direction, photography, texture and motion

## Creative concept: The Artist's House

A contemporary editorial identity with the warmth of a place where art is made and lived with.

### Visual principles

1. **Art leads.** The artwork, artist or experience is the primary subject.
2. **Space creates clarity.** Margins and hierarchy organise information; they are not empty decoration.
3. **Texture supports.** Grain and material details remain secondary.
4. **Contrast gives character.** Balance warm neutrals with a distinctive dark accent.
5. **Consistency allows variety.** Artists bring different visual worlds; the surrounding system stays recognisable.

**Avoid:** spiritual symbols, ornamental luxury motifs, generic paint splashes, faux marble, metallic gradients and excessive decorative framing.

## Logo system

Primary identity: a refined Scintillart wordmark. Use the approved artwork; do not recreate it by typing the name in a similar font.

Required versions: horizontal wordmark; compact or stacked lockup; simplified S symbol; one-colour dark version; one-colour light version. The existing gold emblem remains provisional until simplified and tested for the wider agency identity.

Status: the horizontal wordmark (dark and light, vector) and the S symbol (from the website icon) are supplied in `logos/` and `symbol/`. Descriptor lockups are still to be confirmed and are not part of this pack.

Rules: clear space of at least the capital S height; uncluttered backgrounds; Carbon, Ivory or the approved single-colour version; preserve proportions; no shadows, outlines, glow or texture; no animation of individual letters without an approved motion treatment; no arbitrary badge. Use the simplified symbol when the full name becomes hard to read, and confirm minimum sizes against the final artwork.

## Photography and artwork

**Show:** artists and their individual practices; hands, tools and material processes; meaningful interaction; architecture and settings relevant to the experience; work in progress and finished outcomes; details that demonstrate care.

**Image treatment:** natural-looking skin tones; consistent but restrained colour treatment; accurate artwork reproduction; a balance of wide scenes, portraits and close details; documentary honesty with editorial composition.

**Image rules:** obtain usage permissions; credit artists and image-makers; do not imply partnerships through unrelated venue photography; do not alter artwork without permission; do not use generated imagery as evidence of completed work; obtain permission before featuring identifiable participants.

## Texture

Fine film grain may appear on selected background surfaces. The website's grain is the reference effect (see `texture/README.md`). Keep text and controls crisp, do not apply grain to artwork, photographs or the logo, avoid visible tiling, and remove it where it reduces clarity. Guideline note: the original wording asked for static grain at about 2–4% opacity; the website's grain is stronger and, on screen, jitters slowly, so this wording needs a decision.

## Motion

Restrained transitions that support orientation and atmosphere. No compulsory introduction before content; no scroll hijacking; no flashing or continuous flicker; no decorative effects over forms or practical information; respect reduced-motion preferences.
